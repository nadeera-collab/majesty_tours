Day trips & à la carte

Every service below can be booked on its own or woven into any multi-day itinerary. Private, punctual, island-wide.

Airport Transfers

Pickups, drop-offs and meet-and-greet, any hour, any terminal.

Cruise Shore Excursions

Full-day guided trips from any Sri Lankan port, back before sail-away.

Bird Watching

Sinharaja, Bundala and Kitulgala with specialist local guides.

Camping

Overnight camps in Yala buffer zones and highland forests.

Water Sports

Jet ski, wakeboarding and kite surfing at Negombo, Bentota and Kalpitiya.

Snorkelling & Diving

Coral gardens off Hikkaduwa, the East Coast and Pigeon Island.

Boat & Lagoon Tours

Madu Ganga mangroves, Negombo lagoon and Koggala Lake by boat.

Spice & Herb Gardens

Working spice gardens in Matale and Kandy with guided tastings.

Photography Tours

Golden-hour sessions at temples, tea estates and wildlife hotspots.

Village Experiences

Cooking lessons, paddy farming and life inside a working Sri Lankan village.

Whale Watching

Blue whales, sperm whales and spinner dolphins off Mirissa and the East Coast.

Tea Factory Tours

See the leaf withered, rolled and fired inside a working highland tea factory.

Tea Tastings

Guided cupping sessions comparing highland grades, straight from the source.

Beach Parties

Private bonfires, DJ sets and beachfront celebrations on the southern coast.

Honeymoon Escapes

Private villas, candlelit dinners and secluded beaches for newlyweds.

Down South Beach Trips

Weligama, Mirissa and Tangalle: surf towns and quiet coves south of Galle.

Nightlife

Colombo rooftop bars, beach clubs and live music, arranged for the evening.

All services are private, available standalone or as part of any multi-day journey.
