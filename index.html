Five ways to see the island

Sample routes below each fully customisable, and each one opens to a day-by-day plan. Pricing is per person, private, with your own vehicle and guide.

01
The Cultural Triangle
Sigiriya · Dambulla · Polonnaruwa · Kandy
from $3904 days · private
Day 1

Colombo → Dambulla

Drive into the dry zone. Afternoon at the Dambulla cave temples: five caves of painted Buddhas under a single rock overhang.

Day 2

Sigiriya rock fortress

Climb the Lion Rock at first light, ahead of the heat and the crowds. Frescoes, water gardens, and the view from a 5th-century sky palace.

Day 3

Polonnaruwa by bicycle

The medieval capital, ringed by ruins and reservoirs. Cycle between the stupas and the serene rock-cut Buddhas of Gal Vihara.

Day 4

Kandy & the Tooth Temple

Into the hills to the last royal capital, the Temple of the Tooth, the lake, and an evening of Kandyan drumming.

Sigiriya · Lion Rock
02
Hill Country & Tea
Nuwara Eliya · Ella · Haputale · the blue train
from $3103 days · private
Day 1

Kandy → Nuwara Eliya

Climb through tea estates to 'Little England'. A working factory tour, a cup at the source, and the cool colonial hill station.

Day 2

The train to Ella

One of the world's great rail journeys: open doors, tea-green valleys, and the slow blue carriages through the highlands to Ella. We book the available segment and confirm the latest line status before your trip.

Day 3

Nine Arches & Little Adam's Peak

A dawn walk to the colonial-era viaduct, then an easy summit for the valley laid out below.

Tea country · Nuwara Eliya
03
Wildlife & Safari
Yala · Udawalawe · Wilpattu leopard country
from $2602 days · private
Day 1

Udawalawe elephants

An afternoon game drive among the herds. Udawalawe has some of the most reliable wild elephant sightings on the island.

Day 2

Yala at dawn

First light into Block 1 for the highest leopard density in the world, plus sloth bears, crocodiles and painted storks.

Yala · leopard at water
04
The Southern Coast
Galle · Mirissa whales · Unawatuna · stilt fishers
from $2803 days · private
Day 1

Galle Fort

Sunset on the Dutch ramparts, boutique lanes, and the old lighthouse. Dinner inside the 17th-century walls.

Day 2

Mirissa whales

An early boat for blue whales and spinner dolphins, then the palm-fringed crescent of Mirissa beach.

Day 3

Stilt fishers & Unawatuna

The famous stilt fishers at Koggala, a turtle hatchery, and a last warm swim at Unawatuna bay.

Blue whale · Mirissa

Day trips & à la carte

Every service below can be booked on its own or woven into any multi-day itinerary. Private, punctual, island-wide.

Airport Transfers

Pickups, drop-offs and meet-and-greet, any hour, any terminal.

Cruise Shore Excursions

Full-day guided trips from any Sri Lankan port, back before sail-away.

Bird Watching

Sinharaja, Bundala and Kitulgala with specialist local guides.

Camping

Overnight camps in Yala buffer zones and highland forests.

Water Sports

Jet ski, wakeboarding and kite surfing at Negombo, Bentota and Kalpitiya.

Snorkelling & Diving

Coral gardens off Hikkaduwa, Trincomalee and Pigeon Island.

Boat & Lagoon Tours

Madu Ganga mangroves, Negombo lagoon and Koggala Lake by boat.

Spice & Herb Gardens

Working spice gardens in Matale and Kandy with guided tastings.

Photography Tours

Golden-hour sessions at temples, tea estates and wildlife hotspots.

Village Experiences

Cooking lessons, paddy farming and life inside a working Sri Lankan village.

All services are private, available standalone or as part of any multi-day journey.
