Overview

Sigiriya is a 5th-century rock fortress rising around 200 metres above the surrounding plain, with royal gardens, the famous Mirror Wall and ancient frescoes near its summit — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of Sri Lanka's most recognisable landmarks. Nearby, the Dambulla Royal Cave Temple, also UNESCO-listed, holds a series of Buddhist cave shrines painted and sculpted over more than a thousand years. Both sites sit in the island's Cultural Triangle, roughly 170km from Colombo.
