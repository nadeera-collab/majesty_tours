Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans change. This policy explains what happens if you need to cancel or amend a confirmed booking, and how refunds are handled.

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Cancellation Policy

This Cancellation Policy forms part of our Terms & Conditions. It applies to all confirmed bookings with Majesty Tours and is designed to be fair to you while covering the costs we commit to suppliers on your behalf.

1. How to cancel

To cancel a booking, please notify us in writing by email at info@majestytourssrilanka.com or via WhatsApp. A cancellation takes effect on the date we receive your written notice. Refunds, where due, are calculated by reference to that date and the number of days before your scheduled arrival.

2. Cancellation by you — refund schedule

Unless different terms are stated on your booking confirmation, the following applies to the total tour cost:

Notice before arrival date Cancellation charge Refund of amount paid
More than 30 days Deposit only (25%) Full refund of any amount paid above the deposit
15 – 30 days 50% of total tour cost 50% refund
7 – 14 days 75% of total tour cost 25% refund
Less than 7 days, or no-show 100% of total tour cost No refund

The 25% deposit is non-refundable in all cases, as it covers advance commitments we make to hotels, national parks and transport partners as soon as your booking is confirmed.

3. Non-refundable elements

Some parts of a booking may carry stricter supplier terms and cannot be refunded once confirmed, regardless of when you cancel. These are treated separately from the schedule above and will be identified on your confirmation where they apply. They may include:

  • Peak-season and festive-period hotel bookings with non-refundable rates;
  • Domestic flights and train reservations;
  • Special event tickets, permits or pre-purchased entrance fees;
  • Any supplier deposit that is itself non-refundable.

4. Amendments & postponement

If, instead of cancelling, you wish to change your travel dates or itinerary, we will always try to help. Where an amendment is possible we will re-quote based on the new arrangements and any supplier charges. A postponement requested close to your arrival date may be treated as a cancellation and re-booking under the schedule above.

5. Cancellation by us

We will only cancel your trip in exceptional circumstances. If we cancel for any reason other than force majeure or your failure to pay, you may choose between:

  • An alternative journey of comparable standard (with any price difference refunded or payable); or
  • A full refund of all amounts you have paid to us.

If we have to cancel because a balance was not paid by its due date, the cancellation charges in section 2 will apply as if you had cancelled on that date.

6. Force majeure & events beyond control

Where a trip cannot go ahead because of events beyond our reasonable control — such as natural disasters, extreme weather, epidemics or pandemics, civil unrest, strikes, government action or airport and border closures — we will not be liable to pay compensation. In these situations we will work with our suppliers to recover and refund whatever amounts we reasonably can, and will pass any recovered sums on to you, less any unrecoverable third-party costs. We may instead offer a credit to travel at a later date. We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance to protect against such events.

7. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are made to the original payer, usually by the same method used for payment.
  • Refunds are processed in US Dollars (USD); any exchange-rate difference, bank charges or card processing fees are borne by the client.
  • We aim to process eligible refunds within 14 working days of confirming the amount due, though the time for funds to reach your account depends on your bank or card provider.

8. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects any rights you may have under the laws of Sri Lanka, including the Consumer Affairs Authority Act, No. 9 of 2003. This policy is governed by the laws of Sri Lanka and read together with our Terms & Conditions.
