Overview

Experience the ultimate journey around Sri Lanka on this unforgettable 14-day island adventure. From ancient UNESCO World Heritage Sites and breathtaking hill country landscapes to thrilling wildlife safaris, pristine beaches, marine adventures, scenic train journeys, and vibrant cities, this carefully crafted itinerary showcases the very best the island has to offer. Designed to avoid repeated experiences while maintaining the perfect balance of culture, nature, wildlife, adventure, relaxation, and authentic local encounters, this is the complete Sri Lankan holiday.

Experience the very best of Sri Lanka on one extraordinary journey around the island. Climb the iconic Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress, explore the ancient kingdoms of Polonnaruwa, visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth, witness rescued elephants at Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage, and enjoy the vibrant Kandy Cultural Dance Performance. Travel through the breathtaking tea country aboard the world-famous Blue Train, discover Ella's spectacular mountain scenery, embark on an unforgettable safari in Yala National Park, witness majestic blue whales and spinner dolphins in Mirissa, wander through the historic streets of the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort, explore the spiritual landmarks of Trincomalee, snorkel among colourful coral reefs at Pigeon Island National Park, unwind on the pristine beaches of Pasikuda, and conclude your adventure with a guided tour of Colombo before your departure. Carefully crafted to avoid repetitive experiences while maintaining the perfect balance of culture, heritage, wildlife, adventure, nature, beaches, and relaxation, The Grand Island offers the ultimate Sri Lankan holiday experience.

Best time to go

Because this itinerary crosses both monsoon zones, some stretch of it is in season whenever you travel, so we sequence the East Coast days into the May–September window and the west/south/hill country days into December–March where your dates allow.

Best months for this route: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Grand Island a private tour?

Yes. Every Majesty Tours itinerary, including The Grand Island, is private: your own air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking chauffeur guide, with no other travellers on your schedule.

Can I customise this itinerary?

Yes. This is a sample itinerary: the route, pace, hotel category and stops can all be adjusted to your interests and travel dates. Tell us what you’d like changed when you enquire.

Are hotels included?

Yes. 13 nights of accommodation are included, along with daily breakfast and dinner, as listed above.

Where does the tour start and end?

Pickup and drop-off are at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA). We meet you at arrivals and transfer you back for your departure flight.

Do I get the free Colombo City Tour with this package?

Yes. Every Majesty Tours booking includes a complimentary open-top double-decker sightseeing tour of Colombo at no extra cost.

Is 14 days enough to see all of this without feeling rushed?

Yes. This itinerary is deliberately sequenced to avoid backtracking and repeated experiences, with two or more nights in most stops so each region gets unhurried time rather than a checklist pace.
