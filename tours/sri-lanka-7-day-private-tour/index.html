Best time to go

This route crosses the hill country and the south coast, both of which run on the southwest monsoon, so December to March is the sweet spot, with January to April also excellent in the highlands. July and August are a reasonable second window. Udawalawe is good all year; Yala is at its best from February to July and usually closes block one in September.
