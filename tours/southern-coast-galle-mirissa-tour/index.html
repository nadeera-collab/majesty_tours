Overview

Discover the beauty of Sri Lanka's spectacular southern coastline on an unforgettable journey featuring scenic rivers, diverse wildlife, colonial heritage, pristine beaches, exciting water adventures, and a vibrant city experience. Cruise through the tranquil mangroves of the Madu River, visit a sea turtle conservation project, explore the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort, experience whale watching in Mirissa, enjoy snorkeling and water sports, relax on the golden beaches of Unawatuna, and conclude your journey with a guided tour of Colombo.

Best time to go

The south coast is driest and calmest from December to March — also peak season for whale watching out of Mirissa.

Best months for this route: Jan, Feb, Mar, Oct, Nov, Dec.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Southern Coast a private tour?

Yes. Every Majesty Tours itinerary, including The Southern Coast, is private — your own air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking chauffeur guide, with no other travellers on your schedule.

Can I customise this itinerary?

Yes. This is a sample itinerary — the route, pace, hotel category and stops can all be adjusted to your interests and travel dates. Tell us what you’d like changed when you enquire.

Are hotels included?

Yes. 2 nights of accommodation are included, along with daily breakfast and dinner, as listed above.

Where does the tour start and end?

Pickup and drop-off are at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA). We meet you at arrivals and transfer you back for your departure flight.

Do I get the free Colombo City Tour with this package?

Yes — every Majesty Tours booking includes a complimentary open-top double-decker sightseeing tour of Colombo at no extra cost.

Is whale watching guaranteed?

Sightings on any single trip are never guaranteed since these are wild animals, but Mirissa’s December–April season has some of the most consistent blue whale and spinner dolphin sightings in the world.
