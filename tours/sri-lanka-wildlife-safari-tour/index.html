Overview

Discover the best of Sri Lanka's wildlife and southern coastline on this exciting three-day adventure. Cruise through the tranquil mangrove forests of the Madu River, visit a sea turtle hatchery, and choose between Udawalawe National Park, renowned for its large herds of wild elephants, or Yala National Park, famous for one of the world's highest leopard densities. Complete your journey by exploring the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort and Sri Lanka's vibrant commercial capital before returning to the airport.

Discover Sri Lanka's spectacular wildlife with the flexibility to choose the safari experience that suits you best. Best time to go

Both Udawalawe and the southern coast are driest and best for game viewing from December to March, with Yala’s block-one section typically closed for its annual break in September.

Best months for this route: Jan, Feb, Mar, Oct, Nov, Dec.

Frequently asked questions

Is Wildlife & Safari a private tour?

Yes. Every Majesty Tours itinerary, including Wildlife & Safari, is private: your own air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking chauffeur guide, with no other travellers on your schedule.

Can I customise this itinerary?

Yes. This is a sample itinerary: the route, pace, hotel category and stops can all be adjusted to your interests and travel dates. Tell us what you’d like changed when you enquire.

Are hotels included?

Yes. 2 nights of accommodation are included, along with daily breakfast and dinner, as listed above.

Where does the tour start and end?

Pickup and drop-off are at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA). We meet you at arrivals and transfer you back for your departure flight.

Do I get the free Colombo City Tour with this package?

Yes. Every Majesty Tours booking includes a complimentary open-top double-decker sightseeing tour of Colombo at no extra cost.

Can I choose between Udawalawe and Yala National Park?

Yes. Day 2 can be arranged at either park: Udawalawe for reliably large wild elephant herds, or Yala for one of the world’s highest leopard densities. Let us know your preference when you enquire.
