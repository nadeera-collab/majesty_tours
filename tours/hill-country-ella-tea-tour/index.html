Best time to go

The hill country is cool and scenic year-round, but driest and clearest from January to April, with a second good spell in July–August.

Best months for this route: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hill Country & Tea a private tour?

Yes. Every Majesty Tours itinerary, including Hill Country & Tea, is private — your own air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking chauffeur guide, with no other travellers on your schedule.

Can I customise this itinerary?

Yes. This is a sample itinerary — the route, pace, hotel category and stops can all be adjusted to your interests and travel dates. Tell us what you’d like changed when you enquire.

Are hotels included?

Yes. 3 nights of accommodation are included, along with daily breakfast and dinner, as listed above.

Where does the tour start and end?

Pickup and drop-off are at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA). We meet you at arrivals and transfer you back for your departure flight.

Do I get the free Colombo City Tour with this package?

Yes — every Majesty Tours booking includes a complimentary open-top double-decker sightseeing tour of Colombo at no extra cost.

Is the scenic train journey guaranteed?

Second-class observation tickets on the Nanu Oya → Ella line are booked in advance but remain subject to availability; if seats aren’t available on your date we’ll arrange the same route by road with extra stops at the viewpoints.
