Overview

Discover the untouched beauty of Sri Lanka's breathtaking East Coast on an unforgettable journey filled with pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, marine adventures, wildlife encounters, ancient temples, and relaxing coastal escapes. Visit the UNESCO-listed Dambulla Cave Temple, embark on a seasonal whale and dolphin watching excursion in Trincomalee, explore the sacred Koneswaram Temple overlooking the Indian Ocean, enjoy an exciting wildlife safari, snorkel among vibrant coral reefs at Pigeon Island National Park, relax on the white sandy beaches of Nilaveli and Pasikuda, experience thrilling water sports, and conclude your holiday with a guided tour of Colombo before your departure.

Best time to go

The East Coast runs on the opposite monsoon to the west and south, so it’s at its best from May to September, when the west coast is at its wettest.

Best months for this route: Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep.

Frequently asked questions

Yes. Every Majesty Tours itinerary, including The East Coast Escape, is private: your own air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking chauffeur guide, with no other travellers on your schedule.

Where does the tour start and end?

Do I get the free Colombo City Tour with this package?

Yes. Every Majesty Tours booking includes a complimentary open-top double-decker sightseeing tour of Colombo at no extra cost.

Why is the East Coast best visited at a different time of year than the rest of Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka has two monsoons on opposite cycles. The East Coast (Trincomalee, Pasikuda, Arugam Bay) is driest from May to September, while the west and south coasts and hill country are driest from December to March.
