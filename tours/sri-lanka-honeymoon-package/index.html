Best time to go

Both halves of this itinerary sit on the southwest monsoon’s dry side from December to March: the calmest sea for Mirissa, the clearest skies in the highlands, and the best window for a honeymoon. January to March is the pick of it. If your wedding dates fall between May and September, tell us: we would rebuild the same idea around the East Coast instead rather than send you into the rain.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sri Lanka Honeymoon Package a private tour?

Yes. Like every Majesty Tours itinerary, it is private throughout: your own air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking chauffeur guide, with no other travellers on your schedule.

How is this itinerary put together?

It combines two routes we already run and publish in full: Hill Country & Tea and The Southern Coast. Rather than invent a new itinerary, we sequence those proven routes back to back so the driving makes geographic sense and nothing is repeated.

Can I change the order or swap a section?

Yes. The component routes are the starting point, not a fixed package. Swap one for another of our tours, add or drop a region, or change the pace. Tell us what you have in mind when you enquire.

How is the price calculated?

Sum of Hill Country & Tea (from $590) + The Southern Coast (from $390). The final quote depends on your dates, party size and hotel category, and we confirm it before anything is booked.

What happens on the day the two routes join?

The join becomes a single travel day rather than a departure and a fresh arrival, so you do not go back to the airport in the middle of the trip. That is the one day we adjust when sequencing the plan around your dates.

Are hotels included?

Yes. Accommodation is included throughout, along with daily breakfast and dinner, the private vehicle and chauffeur guide, fuel, parking, highway tolls and government taxes.
