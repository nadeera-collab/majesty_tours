Best time to go

The Cultural Triangle is dry-zone territory and pleasant most of the year, with its best, driest stretch from January to April and again in July–August.

Best months for this route: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Oct, Nov, Dec.

Frequently asked questions

Is The Cultural Triangle a private tour?

Yes. Every Majesty Tours itinerary, including The Cultural Triangle, is private — your own air-conditioned vehicle and English-speaking chauffeur guide, with no other travellers on your schedule.

Can I customise this itinerary?

Yes. This is a sample itinerary — the route, pace, hotel category and stops can all be adjusted to your interests and travel dates. Tell us what you’d like changed when you enquire.

Are hotels included?

Yes. 4 nights of accommodation are included, along with daily breakfast and dinner, as listed above.

Where does the tour start and end?

Pickup and drop-off are at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA). We meet you at arrivals and transfer you back for your departure flight.

Do I get the free Colombo City Tour with this package?

Yes — every Majesty Tours booking includes a complimentary open-top double-decker sightseeing tour of Colombo at no extra cost.

Do we climb Sigiriya Rock ourselves, or is it guided?

Your chauffeur guide accompanies you throughout and can guide the Sigiriya climb, though the pace up the rock is entirely yours — there’s no fixed group schedule.
