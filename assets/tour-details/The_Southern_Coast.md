The Southern Coast

3 Days | 2 Nights | Private Tour

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) → Balapitiya → Kosgoda → Galle → Mirissa → Unawatuna → Colombo → Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA)

Starting From: USD 390 (Private Tour)

Discover the beauty of Sri Lanka’s spectacular southern coastline on an unforgettable journey featuring scenic rivers, diverse wildlife, colonial heritage, pristine beaches, exciting water adventures, and a vibrant city experience. Cruise through the tranquil mangroves of the Madu River, visit a sea turtle conservation project, explore the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort, experience whale watching in Mirissa, enjoy snorkeling and water sports, relax on the golden beaches of Unawatuna, and conclude your journey with a guided tour of Colombo.

Why Choose This Tour?

Experience the very best of Sri Lanka’s Southern Coast in the comfort of a private vehicle with an experienced chauffeur guide. Cruise through the enchanting Madu River, visit a traditional cinnamon island, explore a spice and herbal garden, learn about sea turtle conservation, wander the historic streets of the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort, witness majestic blue whales and spinner dolphins in Mirissa, snorkel in crystal-clear waters, enjoy thrilling water sports, unwind on the golden beaches of Unawatuna, and finish your holiday with a guided tour of Colombo’s iconic landmarks and shopping districts before your airport transfer. Carefully crafted to offer the perfect balance of nature, adventure, heritage, city exploration, and relaxation, this itinerary promises an unforgettable Sri Lankan coastal holiday.
