# The Cultural Triangle

5 Days | 4 Nights | Private Tour

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) → Dambulla → Sigiriya → Polonnaruwa → Kandy → Colombo → Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA)

Starting From: USD 690 (Private Tour)

Discover the timeless beauty of Sri Lanka's Cultural Triangle on a journey through ancient kingdoms, UNESCO World Heritage Sites, breathtaking landscapes, and vibrant local traditions. This carefully designed tour combines history, culture, nature, and city sightseeing, making it the perfect introduction to Sri Lanka.

---

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival | BIA → Dambulla

Welcome to Sri Lanka! Meet your private chauffeur guide at Bandaranaike International Airport and begin your journey to the Cultural Triangle.

Highlights
- Airport Meet & Greet
- Scenic Drive to Dambulla
- Dambulla Royal Cave Temple (UNESCO World Heritage Site)

Overnight: Dambulla / Sigiriya

---

### Day 2 – Sigiriya Heritage Experience

Start your day by climbing one of Sri Lanka's most iconic landmarks before immersing yourself in authentic village life.

Highlights
- Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
- Royal Gardens
- Mirror Wall
- Sigiriya Frescoes
- Sigiriya Village Experience with Traditional Sri Lankan Lunch
- Pidurangala Rock

Overnight: Sigiriya

---

### Day 3 – Polonnaruwa → Kandy

Explore Sri Lanka's medieval capital before travelling to the cultural capital of the island.

Highlights
- Ancient City of Polonnaruwa (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
- Royal Palace
- Sacred Quadrangle
- Gal Vihara
- Rankoth Vehera
- Spice Garden Visit
- Evening Kandy Cultural Dance Performance

Overnight: Kandy

---

### Day 4 – Kandy Sightseeing → Colombo

Visit Sri Lanka's most sacred Buddhist temple before discovering the country's vibrant commercial capital.

Highlights
- Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
- Kandy Lake
- Kandy Viewpoint
- Peradeniya Botanical Garden
- Tea Factory Visit
- Wood Carving Workshop
- Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage

Overnight: Colombo

---

### Day 5 – Colombo City Tour → Departure (BIA)

Highlights
- Colombo City Tour
- Independence Square
- Gangaramaya Temple
- Galle Face Green
- Port City Colombo
- Pettah Market
- Lotus Tower (Photo Stop)
- Shopping
- Transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport
