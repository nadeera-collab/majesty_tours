The East Coast Escape

4 Days | 3 Nights | Private Tour

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) → Dambulla → Trincomalee → Nilaveli → Minneriya → Pigeon Island → Pasikuda → Colombo → Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA)

Starting From: USD 590 (Private Tour)

Discover the untouched beauty of Sri Lanka’s breathtaking East Coast on an unforgettable journey filled with pristine beaches, crystal-clear waters, marine adventures, wildlife encounters, ancient temples, and relaxing coastal escapes. Visit the UNESCO-listed Dambulla Cave Temple, embark on a seasonal whale and dolphin watching excursion in Trincomalee, explore the sacred Koneswaram Temple overlooking the Indian Ocean, enjoy an exciting wildlife safari, snorkel among vibrant coral reefs at Pigeon Island National Park, relax on the white sandy beaches of Nilaveli and Pasikuda, experience thrilling water sports, and conclude your holiday with a guided tour of Colombo before your departure.

Why Choose This Tour?

Experience the untouched paradise of Sri Lanka’s spectacular East Coast in the comfort of a private vehicle with an experienced chauffeur guide. Visit the UNESCO-listed Dambulla Cave Temple, embark on a seasonal whale and dolphin watching excursion in Trincomalee, explore the sacred Koneswaram Temple and the dramatic cliffs of Swami Rock, enjoy an exciting safari in Minneriya National Park, snorkel among colourful coral reefs and tropical marine life at Pigeon Island National Park, unwind on the pristine beaches of Nilaveli and Pasikuda, experience exciting water sports, and conclude your journey with a guided tour of Colombo’s iconic landmarks and shopping districts before your airport transfer. Carefully designed to combine marine adventures, wildlife, heritage, city exploration, and relaxation, this itinerary offers the perfect East Coast holiday.
