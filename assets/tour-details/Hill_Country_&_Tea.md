# Hill Country & Tea

4 Days | 3 Nights | Private Tour

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) → Kandy → Nuwara Eliya → Ella → Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA)

Starting From: USD 590 (Private Tour)

Escape to the cool highlands of Sri Lanka on an unforgettable journey through misty mountains, endless tea plantations, breathtaking train rides, cascading waterfalls, and charming colonial towns. Discover the island's world-famous tea country while enjoying spectacular viewpoints, scenic landscapes, colonial heritage, and authentic local experiences.

---

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival | BIA → Kandy

Welcome to Sri Lanka! Meet your private chauffeur guide at Bandaranaike International Airport and begin your scenic drive into Sri Lanka's beautiful hill country.

Highlights
- Airport Meet & Greet
- Scenic Drive to Kandy
- Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage
- Peradeniya Botanical Garden
- Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic
- Kandy Lake
- Kandy Viewpoint
- Wood Carving Factory
- Evening Kandy Cultural Dance Performance

Overnight: Kandy

---

### Day 2 – Kandy → Nuwara Eliya

Journey through Sri Lanka's scenic hill country, passing lush tea plantations, cascading waterfalls, and misty mountain landscapes. Explore the charming town of Nuwara Eliya, known for its cool climate, colonial architecture, and breathtaking natural beauty.

Highlights
- Tea Factory Tour & Tea Tasting
- Ramboda Waterfall
- Gregory Lake
- Nuwara Eliya City Tour
- Colonial Buildings & Victoria Park (Photo Stops)

Overnight: Nuwara Eliya

---

### Day 3 – Nuwara Eliya → Scenic Train Journey → Ella

Enjoy one of the world's most beautiful train journeys through rolling tea plantations, mist-covered mountains, and picturesque villages before exploring Ella.

Highlights
- Scenic Blue Train Journey (Nanu Oya → Ella)
- Nine Arch Bridge
- Little Adam's Peak
- Ravana Falls
- Flying Ravana Mega Zipline
- Tea Plantation Walk
- Ella Town Exploration
- Relax and Enjoy Ella's Cafés and Mountain Atmosphere

Overnight: Ella

---

### Day 4 – Ella → BIA

After breakfast, begin your scenic return journey to Bandaranaike International Airport.

Highlights
- Beautiful Hill Country Drive
- Scenic Viewpoints Along the Way
- Local Fruit Stalls
- Gem Museum Visit
- Transfer to Bandaranaike International Airport

## Why Choose This Tour?

Experience the very best of Sri Lanka's spectacular hill country in comfort with your own private chauffeur guide. Ride the world-famous Blue Train through breathtaking mountain scenery, visit working tea plantations and factories, stroll through the colonial charm of Nuwara Eliya, and discover Ella's iconic viewpoints, waterfalls, and lush landscapes. Designed for nature lovers and adventure seekers alike, this journey offers the perfect blend of relaxation, scenic beauty, and unforgettable experiences in Sri Lanka's enchanting tea country.
