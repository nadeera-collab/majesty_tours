Wildlife & Safari

3 Days | 2 Nights | Private Tour

Route: Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) → Bentota → Udawalawe or Yala National Park → Galle → Colombo → Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA)

Starting From: USD 390 (Private Tour)

Discover the best of Sri Lanka’s wildlife and southern coastline on this exciting three-day adventure. Cruise through the tranquil mangrove forests of the Madu River, visit a sea turtle hatchery, and choose between Udawalawe National Park, renowned for its large herds of wild elephants, or Yala National Park, famous for one of the world’s highest leopard densities. Complete your journey by exploring the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort and Sri Lanka’s vibrant commercial capital before returning to the airport.

Why Choose This Tour?

Discover Sri Lanka’s spectacular wildlife with the flexibility to choose the safari experience that suits you best. Cruise through the scenic Madu River, visit a sea turtle hatchery, and explore either Udawalawe National Park, renowned for its magnificent elephant herds, or Yala National Park, famous for its elusive leopards and rich biodiversity. Complete your journey with a visit to the UNESCO-listed Galle Fort and a short Colombo City Tour before your departure. Carefully crafted to combine wildlife, nature, coastal beauty, heritage, and city sightseeing, this private tour offers an unforgettable Sri Lankan experience in just three days.
